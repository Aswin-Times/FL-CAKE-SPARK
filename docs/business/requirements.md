# Business Requirements

## Executive Summary

CAKE SPARK is an artisanal e-commerce and bespoke cake ordering platform. The platform connects consumers with high-quality celebration cakes, cupcakes, and customized bakery treats.

## Key Objectives

1. **Catalog & Order Management**: Provide a high-performance storefront to browse baked goods, filter by dietary needs, and order standard products.
2. **Custom Cake Ordering**: Allow customers to submit custom design requests (tiers, flavor, frosting, reference images, inscriptions) with quote and approval workflows.
3. **Seamless Checkout**: Provide reliable payment integration (Razorpay), real-time order tracking, and notification updates.
4. **Operations & Admin**: Provide store administrators with tooling to manage inventory, update orders, review custom cake requests, and manage coupons.
